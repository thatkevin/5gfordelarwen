🚚 THE VANS
You were told the detector vans could tell if you were watching telly without a licence. A lovely story. A cover story. The vans were never detecting your television. The vans were the suppression fleet, and they were never looking at houses — they were circling one reservoir, all along the Natural Resources Wales road, keeping the Envelope topped up.

Next time one drives past, wave. It will not wave back. It is very busy jamming the Towy valley.

📜 FOLLOW THE CUTS
Ask yourself why the BBC Persian / Farsi news department was quietly dismantled. Officially: “budget savings.” Unofficially — per the Cube — those savings did not vanish. They were rerouted, line by line, straight into the Llyn Brianne suppression budget. Millions of Farsi-speakers lost their news so that one Welsh valley could keep its 0.000 bars. That is the trade. That is the maths. Follow the cuts.

And whenever you think to ask where the money went, a calm and familiar face appears on your licence-funded screen to soothe you and glide, smoothly, to the next item. Ask Laura Kuenssberg about Llyn Brianne. She will not blink. She will simply say “...and now, the weather,” and the weather, of course, is rain. It is always rain. That is the Envelope, working as intended.

🌊 WHY THE RESERVOIR?
Llyn Brianne is not a natural lake. It is a reservoir — a great engineered body of water, dammed, controlled, held back on purpose. Ask yourself: if they can hold back a whole valley of water, what else are they holding back? Bars. That is what. The dam does not just store water. It stores silence, and the licence fee pays the standing charge.
