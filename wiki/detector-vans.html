Detector vans

From The Weird World Wiki, the free encyclopaedia

The detector vans were long said to identify unlicensed televisions. Under the licence-fee theory they are re-interpreted as the suppression fleet: never looking at houses, but circling one reservoir along the Dam-Wall Road, keeping the Envelope topped up.
