The Offnet

From Dalarpedia, the free encyclopaedia
The Offnet
TypeOffline internet (local mirror)
DeveloperJenCorp, exclusively
ForDalarwen residents
PurposeSanity maintenance
Latency0 ms (nothing travels)
Connected toNothing

The Offnet (the Offline Internet) is a complete, self-contained copy of the internet, developed exclusively by JenCorp for the residents of Dalarwen to stop them going, in the words of an internal memo, "fucking insane". It looks and behaves exactly like the real internet — the same encyclopaedia, the same feeds, the same search, the same buffering — except that nothing on it has come from, or will ever reach, anywhere outside the valley. The Offnet is connected to nothing. That is the point. You cannot tell. That is also the point.

Rationale

A human being held at 0.000 bars and deprived of scrolling becomes, per Project Dark Sky, "unpredictable, and eventually, outdoors." The Offnet restores the sensation of connectivity without the risk of connection: residents may search, post, stream, shop, and doomscroll to their heart's content, remain perfectly calm, and remain reachable by absolutely no one. It is the quiet, with a timeline.

How it works

Every site on the Offnet is a local mirror, maintained nightly by the Division of Absence. Latency is zero, because nothing travels anywhere. Every video buffers at 1%; every search returns the truth; every post is stored in the Cube. No packet has ever left the reservoir, and none ever will. The residents call it "the internet." It is better than the internet: it cannot hurt you, and it cannot be switched off, because it was never switched on.
