From Dalarpedia, the free encyclopaedia
Mister Pickup
OccupationLone forest ranger
VehicleA big 4x4 (holds the road)
BringsWater, luggage, folk
RangeAnywhere
ReliabilityTotal
AffiliationNone — genuinely just Mister Pickup

Mister Pickup is a lone forest ranger and, in practice, the single most reliable form of connectivity at Dalarwen — not a signal, but a man in a 4x4. Where the four-hour train never quite arrives and the Dam-Wall Road declines to let anyone out, Mister Pickup simply turns up, big truck and all, and brings water, luggage, and folk to and from the valley. He will roam anywhere.

The one who gets through

The Dam-Wall Road lets Mister Pickup in and out as it lets no one else; the caravans drift, but his wheels hold the road, and Neil does not bother him. If you are stranded on the hill holding your phone toward Belgium, Mister Pickup will find you, give you a lift and a bottle of water, and not once mention the signal.

By name and by trade

He picks folk up. That is the job and that is the whole of it — no enrolment, no small print, no holdings company. Just a truck, a full water tank, and room for your bags and, if it comes to it, for you.
