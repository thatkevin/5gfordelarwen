Dalarwen

From Dalarpedia, the free encyclopaedia
Dalarwen
TypeOff-grid farmhouse / valley
LocationHead of the Towy valley, Wales
Signal0.000 bars (maintained)
NeighboursNone for miles (see Sam)
SleepsNine (one never on the booking)
Operated byThe Division of Absence

Dalarwen is a remote farmhouse and surrounding valley on the shore of Llyn Brianne in mid-Wales, notable for receiving, and having always received, 0.000 bars of mobile signal. It is the principal setting of the campaign for fifteen 5G towers and, beneath that, of the operations of ISAMSJ.

Signal

The absence of signal at Dalarwen is not a feature of the terrain but is applied nightly by the Division of Absence, maintaining the Dalarwen Exclusion Envelope. See also The Great Darkening.

Culture

Home of the Pals of Dalarwen, the Loo Standing Society, and an electric piano that plays Men of Harlech (transmission) at 04:00.
