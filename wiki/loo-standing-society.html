The Loo Standing Society
MottoNil Signum, Sed Stamus
FoundedMMXIX
PostureStanding (mandatory)

The Loo Standing Society (est. 2019) is a Dalarwen institution founded on the observation that the smallest room, when one stands upon the cistern, achieves a signal of precisely zero bars — the same as everywhere else. Its members stand anyway, as a matter of principle and posture.
