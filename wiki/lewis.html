Lewis is a resident of the Dalarwen valley and the campaign's most sympathetic figure. He possesses thirteen finished videos and no signal with which to upload them. He has been observed on The Hill since Tuesday, phone aloft, upload stalled at 1%.

The Last Towa

In the final scenario, Lewis receives five bars just as the valley floods, and must choose between destroying the tower to save Dalarwen or waiting for his thirteenth video to upload. See the study.
