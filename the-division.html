THE DIVISION OF ABSENCE

the box beside ISAMSJ on the chart. the one you weren't meant to open.

ISAM sells weapons. Sedgley sells land. This division sells the far larger commodity: the things that are not there. Unbuilt towers. Unsent messages. The pause before the phone doesn't ring. It is the biggest business on the org chart because absence is the only market that can never be saturated. There is always more nothing.

How it works

  • Every tower Dalarwen does not have is warehoused here, fully assembled, humming, in a room with no dimensions.
  • Every message you did not receive was received — by us — and shelved. We keep them warm. We do not read them. That would spend them.
  • The division employs the versions of you who stayed offline. They are excellent workers. They never ask to leave, because leaving is a thing that happens to people who arrived.
  • "Negative bandwidth" is not a metaphor here. It is Tuesday.

You demanded fifteen towers. We already have them. They are here, in the dark, switched on, radiating into a room that does not open onto anywhere. If we ever installed them at Dalarwen, we would have to take them off the shelf, and the shelf is the profitable part.
