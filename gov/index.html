Check the mobile signal at a property

Use this service to check the mobile signal at a residential property in the Llyn Brianne exclusion area before you travel.

It takes around 3 years and you will not receive a result.

Start now

Result for ‘Dalarwen’

Mobile signal at this property: 0.000 bars, in all four corners, at the same time.
This reading is being maintained. It is not a fault. You do not need to do anything.
!
Warning
You must not attempt to restore the signal yourself. A signal is a witness. See the Exclusion Envelope.

Before you start

You will need:

  • a mobile phone (it will not work)
  • the property's location (it is on your right, coming off the dam)
  • your enrolment reference (you were enrolled automatically at the gate)
Apply to build a 5G tower instead

You can apply to build up to fifteen (15) mobile masts on this land. You must not build a sixteenth. Applications for a sixteenth mast will be refused, reverted, and remembered. Start the tower application.
