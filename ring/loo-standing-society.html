The Loo Standing Society

Established MMXIX — Dalarwen, Cambria

"Nil Signum, Sed Stamus"
(No Signal, But We Stand)

Our Purpose

The Society was founded upon a single, dignified observation: that the smallest room in the house, when one is standing, and only when standing, upon the cistern, achieves a signal of precisely zero bars — the very same as sitting, and indeed as every other corner of the property. We stand nonetheless. It is a matter of principle, and of posture.

Bylaws (Abridged)

  1. A Member shall, upon entering the loo, raise the telephone aloft in the customary manner.
  2. A Member shall observe the reading (nil) with equanimity.
  3. At no point shall a Member sit down and admit defeat. We are a standing society.
  4. The phrase "I'll text you when I get a bar" is forbidden within the Society's premises.
  5. The Society endorses the campaign for fifteen towers, whilst noting, with regret, that it would render our raison d'être obsolete.

Patrons
