Welcome, friend

When the carrier quietly downgraded Dalarwen to Silence Premium — unlimited nothing, forever — many of us felt lost. This is a safe, calm space to share that. No judgement. No notifications. No signal to send them anyway.

24-Hour Helpline: we set one up. It cannot ring. Please picture it ringing, and picture us answering warmly. That is the service, and it helps more than you'd think.

You may be a Silence Premium survivor if…

  • You have held a phone toward Belgium and felt hope.
  • You refresh a page that will never load, for company.
  • You have told someone "I'll text you when I get a bar," and meant it, in 2019.
  • You have made peace with the quiet, then un-made it, then made it again.

Weekly Meetings

Thursdays, by the fire. We sit. We do not scroll. Someone brings a board-game. It is, quietly, the best evening of the week. Newcomers welcome; bees welcome; Lewis especially welcome.
