Backpacking The Signal

~ my Peru diaries ~ a webring travel journal ~

📷 🏔 🦙 📱

Hiya! Welcome to my travel page. I'm doing a gap year following the ancient signal lines round the world (long story). Peru was the BEST. Keeping this diary offline because, ironically, half these places have no reception — just like that Welsh valley everyone bangs on about.

Day 12 — The Nazca Lines

Took the little plane over the Nazca Lines today. Massive drawings in the desert you can only see from the sky — a hummingbird, a monkey, a spider. The guide said "nobody knows what they're for." Mate. They're antennae. Giant ground antennae, same as the pyramids. The whole pampa is a transmitter. Had four bars up there. FOUR. In a desert. And Dalarwen has zero. Makes you think. NAZCA ✓

Day 15 — Machu Picchu

Cloud forest, llamas everywhere (rude, but I love them). Got to the top and — get this — full signal. Up a mountain, in the clouds, 15th-century Inca city: full bars. Sent 40 photos. The Incas clearly knew something we forgot. Meanwhile back home Lewis is on a hill in the rain at 1%. Bless him.

Day 18 — Lake Titicaca

Highest navigable lake in the world (a reservoir's cousin, if you will). Total peace. Reminded me a lot of the descriptions of Dalarwen — that same big quiet. Maybe the two places are on the same ley line. The Norfolk lot reckon so.

Day 21 — met an actual genius (and her dog)

Okay so you will NOT believe who I ran into on a ridge outside the pampa. Dr. Melonsis Borealis — "Isis" to her mates — the one who wrote that electric sheep paper everyone in the webring goes on about. She was out here with a whole field lab: jars of green stuff, a blackboard covered in sheep and spirals, and that glowing orb she says is a wormhole she is "keeping an eye on." I did not ask.

And she has a DOG. This is Lemon. Lemon is a very good girl and honestly the most qualified member of the expedition — she has her own field-research harness and a wormhole detector strapped to it, which apparently beeps near thin spots in reality. It beeped the whole time I was talking to them, which Isis said was "probably you, don't worry about it." Lemon found four bars by sitting on a specific rock. Genuinely a better signal engineer than my carrier. LEMON ✓

What I learned

The signal was always there. Peru kept it. Wales gave it away. The campaign for fifteen towers is basically an attempt to remember what the Nazca already knew. Sign me up.

Ta for reading my diary! Next stop: the fens. · home
