Nan's Guide To Turning The Wifi On

☕ 📱 ❓ 📵

Hello dears. My grandson keeps saying I need to "get online". I have made this helpful guide. It has not worked yet but I am STAYING POSITIVE. 😊

STEP 1. Locate the little box with the lights. (We do not have one. I have drawn one on a napkin. It has no lights.)
STEP 2. Press the button. Any button. Press them all. Press them again but crossly.
STEP 3. Hold the telephone up. Higher. Toward Belgium, apparently. Wave it about like a jam jar catching a wasp.
STEP 4. If no bars appear (there will be no bars), put the kettle on. This does not help the Wi-Fi but it helps Nan.
STEP 5. The nice man on the telephone shop said we need a "5G tower". We need FIFTEEN, apparently. I have written to my MP on actual paper.
